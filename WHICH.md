# Top 3 Most Popular CMS Platforms

A brief overview of the three most widely used Content Management Systems today:

### 1. WordPress
* **Best For:** General websites, blogs, and corporate sites.
* **Key Strengths:** Powers over 40% of all websites. It is open-source, highly customizable, and offers a massive ecosystem of plugins and themes for any functionality.

### 2. Shopify
* **Best For:** E-commerce stores.
* **Key Strengths:** The industry leader for online retail. It provides an all-in-one, fully hosted platform with built-in payment processing, inventory management, and security.

### 3. Wix
* **Best For:** Beginners and small businesses.
* **Key Strengths:** Extremely user-friendly drag-and-drop website builder that includes hosting and templates, making it fast and simple to create a site without technical skills.
